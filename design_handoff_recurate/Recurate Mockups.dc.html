1Direction 1a in the Claude Design language — cream paper, sepia ink, one terracotta accent. Songs shell (default screen) and the Playlists hub.
Direction. Cream paper, sepia ink, one terracotta accent used like punctuation. Serif (Newsreader) for page titles only; Inter for every control; JetBrains Mono for data. Hairlines instead of borders, no shadows, no gradients. 1000×700 frames — every bottom control stays inside. Cover art = flat sunk-paper tiles until real embedded art is wired in. Light is the default; the same tokens carry a warm-charcoal dark mode (data-theme="dark"). Tokens and component specs: README.md, brand spec: docs/claude-design.md.
